## What we did today

1. **Unsupervised cluster interpretability** (`utils/interpretability.py`,
   wired into `02_unsupervised_clustering.ipynb` and `03_bertopic.ipynb`).
   Your mentor wanted to see *what* each cluster actually contains (top
   TF-IDF terms, example docs, majority-true-label + purity), not just
   aggregate metrics like ACC/NMI. Also fixed BERTopic's vectorizer to
   filter stopwords (was surfacing "the, to, of, in" as topic words).
   Output: `results/clusters_*.csv` per method.

2. **Label propagation** — new semi-supervised method
   (`utils/label_propagation.py`, `notebooks/08_label_propagation.ipynb`).
   Embeds the 5% labeled seed + unlabeled pool with MiniLM, builds a k-NN
   graph, propagates labels via `sklearn.semi_supervised.LabelSpreading` —
   no fine-tuning loop at all. Chose this over the other candidates in
   `docs/semi_supervised_methods.md` because it's cheap and reuses existing
   embedding infra. Result: 87.2% label accuracy, 100% coverage.

3. **Fixed the pseudo-labeling stall.** At the original settings
   (`confidence_threshold=0.90`, `CLASSIFIER_SAMPLE_SIZE=150`), self-training
   absorbed **zero** pseudo-labels — the DistilBERT model, fine-tuned on
   only ~152 rows, never got confident enough to cross 0.90, even though its
   predictions were often right (82.8% test accuracy). Two experiments in
   `notebooks/05_pseudo_labeling.ipynb`:
   - Lowered `confidence_threshold` to 0.60 (same 150-row sample): confirmed
     threshold was the real bottleneck — 72.8% coverage, 91.2% label
     accuracy, test accuracy rose to 84.9%.
   - Also widened to a notebook-local `PSEUDO_LABEL_SAMPLE_SIZE=400` (scoped
     to this notebook only, not `utils.config.CLASSIFIER_SAMPLE_SIZE`, so
     `06_full_supervised_baseline.ipynb` is unaffected): reached 96.75%
     coverage in a **single round**, 90.4% label accuracy, **88.6% test
     accuracy — now the best method in the whole comparison table**, ahead
     of `full_supervised` (85.5%) and `label_propagation` (87.2%).

4. Ran the full pipeline (00→08) end to end multiple times to keep
   `results/comparison_table.csv` current after each change.
